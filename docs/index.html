Fly circuit exploration

Reading the fruit-fly navigation literature against two synapse-level wiring diagrams, and asking what the uncharacterised neurons are for.

Circuit view
Leaving food and coming back, drawn start to finish in the order information must flow, with every node coloured by evidence: proven, physiology only, structural only, missing, controversial. The eight findings are pinned where they sit.
List view
Self-contained explainers, ranked by how much they should change current thinking. Each has background, a figure, the evidence, why it matters and what would settle it.

Source and full analysis notes: github.com/pwang724/fly-circuit-exploration. Data: MaleCNS v1.0 and hemibrain v1.2 connectomes.
