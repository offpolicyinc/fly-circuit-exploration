The home vector is probably stored in synapses, not in activity. Several hΔ sites have the ingredients, and none of them inverts it: the return needs hΔM.

Flies add up their movements and walk back to food, but no neuron has been shown to do the adding. A systematic search of every fan-shaped-body population finds that none has recurrent wiring strong enough to hold a vector as ongoing activity. What the wiring does have, at one place, is everything a synaptic integrator needs: the travel-direction bump (hΔB) synapsing column by column onto hΔJ; dopamine cells driven by walking (FB4M, FB1H) on every hΔJ cell; a reward octopamine neuron (OA-VPM3) on the same cells; and an output from hΔJ into the goal population that is anatomically flipped by 180°, so a stored displacement comes out pointing home. A closed-loop model built from the measured wiring returns to food; remove the flip and it walks away.

Correction (2026-09-12). The first version of this page said hΔJ's 194° output "flips the stored displacement toward home". Anchoring every phase to two published conventions (hΔ labels are dendritic columns, Hulse 2021; the hΔB travel bump sits on its axon, Lyu 2022) shows that hΔB's axon lands on the axonal arbor of hΔA, hΔH, hΔG and hΔI and on both arbors of hΔJ, so those routes deliver the travel direction to FC2/PFL3 with its sign preserved (Δ = +15° to +21°), and hΔJ carries both signs (its stored vector cancels to 14 % of ideal). The closed-loop model still requires a net inversion for return; the wiring provides it downstream of the goal, in hΔM (FC2 → hΔM → PFL3 at 178°) or the hΔK/PFGs loop. The candidate store is now the family hΔH, hΔA, hΔI, hΔG (and the PFN→hΔB synapses), with hΔH and hΔA the cleanest; the walking-driven dopamine (FB4M/FB1H), the OA-VPM3 reward input, and the negative result on activity-based storage all stand. Full derivation: exhaustive-search.md.

Prior work found in the sweep. Goulard, Heinze & Webb 2023 modelled dopamine-gated synaptic goal memory at tangential→hΔ/PFN synapses. Gorko 2026 (Kim lab) showed experimentally, in the PFL1/FC1/FB2B system, that a stored FB vector is not encoded in presynaptic calcium activity and that FC1 drives a sinusoidal cAMP pattern in FB2B axons: the first evidence that a fan-shaped-body vector memory is synaptic or second-messenger based, in a different column system from the one analysed here.

Background

What the behaviour proves
After leaving sugar, flies search centred on it in darkness, re-zero the centre when they find food again, and lose a learned distance when PFNd/PFNv/hΔB/PFR are silenced (Kim 2017; Behbahani 2021; D'Atri 2025). Some population must accumulate travel over tens of seconds.
Two ways to accumulate
In activity: a population whose recurrent wiring lets a bump persist and grow (a ring or line attractor). In synapses: a bump that keeps potentiating its own output synapses while a modulator permits it, so the weight pattern becomes the sum. The second was proposed in outline by Hulse 2021 and Maimon & Abbott 2026.
hΔ neurons
Fan-shaped-body cells with input in one column and output half a turn away. Corrected phase measurements show every hΔ output landing 193–202° from its input. The inversion is anatomy, not computation.
OA-VPM3
A pair of octopamine neurons that innervate the mushroom body and are implicated in sweet-taste reward. Hulse 2021 noted in one sentence that OA-VPM3 is an octopaminergic FB tangential; its targets in the vector system had not been described.
What was already proposed
Maimon & Abbott 2026 (their Figure 5) propose that a vector memory could be "a sinusoidally varying synaptic weight profile across a columnar population" presynaptic to FC2, written by cAMP/monoamines, with tangential neurons selecting among memories. Hulse 2021 offered tangential-neuron plasticity and hΔ column pairs as options. Insect models (Stone 2017; Le Moël 2019; Goulard 2023) have used both activity- and plasticity-based stores. The mechanism type is therefore not new. What no one had done is test which of the two the fly's wiring supports, or name the cells.

What is new here

  • The negative result: recurrence is too weak in every columnar population for an activity-based integrator, which rules out one of the two options Maimon & Abbott left open.
  • hΔJ as the site. It appears in Hulse 2021 only in a table and one anatomical sentence; no function had been proposed.
  • FB4M and FB1H as walking-gated dopamine for translation, the analogue of ExR2 for rotation. Listed as dopaminergic by Hulse 2021, never assigned a role.
  • OA-VPM3's projections onto hΔB, PFR_a, hΔJ, hΔH and PFGs, and its Kenyon-cell input, and the proposal that it is the re-zero signal.
  • That hΔJ's own 194° output performs the inversion into a return goal, with a closed-loop control showing the inversion is necessary.

Why it matters

  • It fills the three red boxes with one mechanism. Integrate (weights accumulate), store the zero (OA-VPM3 erases at food), invert (hΔJ anatomy). Arrival falls out: the weight vector shrinks to zero, the goal disappears, local search begins.
  • It reuses the compass's own plasticity logic. ExR2 dopamine gates ring-to-compass learning by rotational speed (Fisher 2022). FB4M is the translational analogue: driven by the velocity system, delivered to the population that reads travel direction.
  • It reinterprets the other candidates. PFR receives inverted travel direction and non-inverted velocity, so it reports heading-versus-travel mismatch, not position. FR1 accumulates a scalar for the mushroom body. hΔA/hΔI hand PFL3 the inverted travel direction as a goal-free "turn back" signal.

What would settle it

  • Image hΔH and hΔA (and hΔJ) during the 1D re-zeroing task: predict a bump pointing along the outbound displacement in these cells, amplitude growing with distance, collapsing at re-zero; predict its inverse in PFL3 via hΔM.
  • Silence OA-VPM3 during local search: predict search that no longer centres on the food, with odometry intact.
  • Block dopamine signalling or cAMP in hΔJ: predict loss of the return vector with hΔB travel-direction coding intact.
  • Pair OA-VPM3 activation with walking in one direction: predict a displaced search centre with no food present.
  • Silence hΔM during a return versus during menotaxis: the return-path reading predicts return fails and heading maintenance survives.
