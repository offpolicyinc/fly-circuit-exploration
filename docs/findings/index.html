What the wiring diagrams say about fly navigation

Nine findings from analysing (novelty-audited 2026-09-12; kickers on each page state what was already in print) the MaleCNS (2026) and hemibrain (2020) synapse-level connectomes against the published circuit models, ranked by how much they should change current thinking. Each page is self-contained: background, figure, evidence, why it matters, what would settle it. Everything beyond the synapse counts is a hypothesis.

Against the literature

1. The compass has a built-in brake that no published model includestwo brainssimulated
The shifter neurons that rotate the compass get three times more input from the position they write to than from the position they read from. Both sides do it, so it anchors the bump; in simulation rotation collapses.
2. The turn command is reprocessed before it reaches the legswhole graphmostly in Rayshubskiy 2025 / Liao 2025
The published PFL3 → DNa02 link is 1.5 % of DNa02's input. Most of the steering signal goes to inhibitory LAL interneurons that fan out to five descending neurons; Feng's steering hierarchy gets nothing from PFL.

Missing computation

9. The home vector is probably stored in synapses at hΔB → hΔ(H, A, I, G); the return needs hΔMsimulatedhypothesis, corrected
No population has recurrence for an activity integrator. Several hΔ sites carry hΔB input, walking-driven dopamine and the reward octopamine neuron OA-VPM3, but every one delivers the stored displacement sign-preserved; only hΔM (and hΔK/PFGs) invert. Return requires that inversion in the closed-loop model.
3. PFR is not the integrator; it reads as a heading-versus-travel mismatchsimulated
With excitatory signs PFR only relays travel direction, and its two velocity inputs arrive in opposite phase (PFNd ~21°, hΔB ~195°), cancelling during straight walking.

Unknown unknowns and omitted inputs

4. The learning centre and the navigation system are wired together, three waysscreenroutes 1, 3 in Hulse/Li
MBON21/09 → FB4R → hΔB; MBON25/34 → FB4G/H → hΔA → PFL3; and hΔB → FR1 → MBON30/PPL1 dopamine. The bridges Chen 2024's result requires, all uncharacterised.
5. The navigation goal has a two-way line to the egg-laying circuitscreenforward edge published 2025
FC2 and its FS1A readouts send ~3,500 synapses to oviIN, the egg-laying gate, which projects back into the goal layer.
6. hΔM inverts the goal into PFL3: the candidate return pathphase measuredre-ranked up
FC2 → hΔM → PFL3 at 178° is the only inverter downstream of the goal. Since every stored-displacement route reaches FC2 sign-preserved, this is what would turn "where I have gone" into "go back".
7. The velocity signals come from cells nobody has namedscreen
PS196_b feeds the rotational (GLNO) and translational (FB3A) velocity inputs plus ExR2/ExR4; FB3A carries an ascending signal into PFNd and supplies 12 % of its input.

Prediction from a published mechanism

8. Compass learning should be cue-specificmeasureddata in Plitt 2025; prediction new
EL octopamine feedback concentrates on ER4d/ER3p/ER3d/ER2_c and barely reaches ER4m or ER6, which get direct EPG feedback instead. ER5 (sleep) is EL's largest input.
