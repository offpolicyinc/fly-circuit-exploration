What the wiring diagrams say about fly navigation

Four findings that survived an exhaustive pass over every fan-shaped-body population and a literature sweep for prior claims (2026-09-12), from the MaleCNS (2026) and hemibrain (2020) connectomes. Each page is self-contained and states what was already in print. A fifth page collects results that turned out to be published.

What this site is. A connectome-mining exercise done with an LLM over two days in September 2026, audited afterwards. Nothing here is an experiment. Each finding page opens with a box separating prior work, connectome observations made here, toy simulations, and hypotheses. Candidate neurons for vector memory were proposed by Maimon & Abbott 2026 and two have since been recorded (Janke 2025; Avritzer 2026); the contribution here is the wiring screen that ranks the unrecorded ones. Corrections are logged in the audit.

The missing middle

1. The home vector may be stored in synapse strengths, at hΔB's synapses onto two unrecorded hΔ typestwo brainssimulatedhypothesis
Two hΔ integrators have been recorded (hΔG, Janke 2025; hΔA, Avritzer 2026): both leaky, both in activity, seconds long. hΔH and hΔI carry column-matched hΔB input and cell-covering dopamine and have not been recorded. Recurrence counts cannot decide between activity and synaptic storage.
2. Every store delivers the displacement sign-preserved; hΔM and hΔI are the only inverters, so they are the return pathphases measuredsimulated
Anchored to Hulse 2021 and Lyu 2022 conventions, hΔB lands on the axonal arbor of each store, so FC2 receives "where I have gone". FC2 → hΔM → PFL3 at 178°, and hΔA → hΔI → PFL3 at 195°, turn it into "go back". One memory: dispersal read directly, return read through hΔM or hΔI.

Unnamed inputs

3. The velocity signals come from cells nobody has namedtwo brains
PS196_b (GLNO's largest input, Hulse 2023) also feeds ExR2, ExR4, LPsP and FB3A and is driven by the ascending neuron AN07B037; FB3A pools visual, antennal and ascending self-motion signals into 12 % of PFNd's input.

Against the models

4. The compass has a built-in brake that textbook models leave outtwo brainssimulated
The shifter neurons get three times more compass input from the position they write to than from the position they read from, on both sides, so they anchor the bump. In simulation rotation collapses. Hulse 2021 saw the dense EB interconnection; the ratio, placement and consequence are new.
