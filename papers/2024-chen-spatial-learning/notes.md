# Chen et al. (2024 preprint): spatial learning in feature-impoverished arenas uses self-deposited scents plus self-motion

[bioRxiv 10.1101/2024.09.28.615625](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.09.28.615625) · [Captured full text](biorxiv-fulltext-v1.md) · [Exact reading coverage](reading-log.md)

## What this contributes to a complete navigation account

A high-throughput spatial learning task (single flies in small featureless chambers, reward at a hidden goal location) shows that flies learn to return to the goal without visual landmarks, but not by path integration alone. Flies mark the rewarded location with self-deposited cuticular hydrocarbons (tricosene/pentacosene-type compounds), assign positive value to the scent through the mushroom body (MB manipulations and Orco loss impair learning), and combine the scent cue with self-motion cues: silencing PFNd neurons, which encode egocentric translational velocity, impairs learning, whereas EPG (compass) and PFR manipulations do not. When prominent external olfactory landmarks are added, flies switch strategy and prioritise the external landmarks over self-generated marks.

## Why it matters for the circuit program

- Directly challenges "pure path integration" interpretations of return-to-goal behavior in enclosed arenas (Kim & Dickinson 2017; Stern 2019; Behbahani 2021): self-marking is a cue that oenocyte ablation may not fully remove and that experiments must control (Titova 2023 raised the same issue).
- The PFNd requirement without an EPG requirement suggests a body-centric odometer or short-range self-motion memory, not a compass-referenced home vector, is used at this scale; consistent with D'Atri 2025's odometer and with the Maimon & Abbott 2026 caution that fly vector memory is not established.
- MB involvement echoes Stern 2019 (MB ablation reduces learning) and gives it an olfactory reading.

## Caveats for synthesis

- Preprint read from bioRxiv HTML; figures viewed in browser but not saved; supplementary material not obtained.
- Small-arena task; larger, ventilated arenas may weight self-motion more.
- Silencing PFNd affects walking-related signals broadly; the specific computation (distance? direction?) is not identified.

## Remaining questions

1. What does PFNd contribute when EPG is dispensable: a distance count, a turning rule, or velocity gating of MB learning?
2. Do flies in Kim & Dickinson's large dark arena also rely on marks (their oenocyte-ablation result argues against, but see Titova 2023)?
3. How the MB scent value and the PFN self-motion signal converge (CX? LAL?).
