# Janke 2025: hΔG is a leaky distance integrator that resets at food (abstract only)

Claims, from the abstract: hΔG activity drops dramatically when the fly encounters a sugar drop; after this reset the mean activity rises over many minutes, and the amplitude of a sinusoidal bump grows and shrinks on the seconds timescale, tracking the distance the fly walked in the past few seconds. Calcium imaging and optogenetics argue the bump is built by integrating synaptic input from vΔE. A formal model combines a minutes-long baseline rise, a vΔE-driven slow-down of that rise, and a continuous leak that preserves the sinusoidal shape but makes the signal an imperfect path integral. The connectome shows multiple vΔ→hΔ circuits across layers; varying leak and ramp rates could give a repertoire of path-integrated signals.

Bearing on this repository: hΔG was one of four candidate synaptic store sites in finding 1 and OA-VPM3 was proposed as its reset. The thesis shows hΔG integrates in activity, leakily, over seconds, and resets at sugar; the reset carrier is not identified in the abstract. In the MaleCNS graph vΔE is hΔG's largest columnar input (2,405 synapses), consistent with the vΔE→hΔG claim. The finding has been rewritten to treat hΔG as recorded and to restrict the synaptic-store candidates to hΔH and hΔI.
